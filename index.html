Who we are

A logistics partner built for a moving continent

GraLex Logistique connects markets, businesses and people across Benin, Nigeria and the wider West African corridor — blending local expertise with world-class operational standards.

  • Nationwide & cross-border reach — one network from first mile to final mile.
  • Insured, tracked cargo — full visibility from pickup to delivery.
